# Indian fuel outlet operating models

Payanam 360 treats the retailer, operating model and branded outlet programme as different facts.

| Code | Meaning | Product display rule |
|---|---|---|
| COCO | Company Owned, Company Operated | Only with official OMC or reviewed outlet evidence |
| CODO | Company Owned, Dealer Operated | Only with official OMC or reviewed outlet evidence |
| DODO | Dealer Owned, Dealer Operated | Only with official OMC or reviewed outlet evidence |
| CLDO | Company Leased, Dealer Operated | Only where the source uses this classification |
| UNKNOWN | Not verified | Default; never inferred from the forecourt brand |

The model is not a universal quality score. A COCO badge describes ownership/operation and evidence, not a guarantee of fuel stock, service quality or purity. Availability and price remain separately sourced observations with their own timestamps.

## Retailers and programmes

The retailer registry is open-ended and can represent IndianOil, BPCL, HPCL, Nayara Energy, Jio-bp, Shell and other public, private, joint-venture or regional retailers. It is not limited to a hard-coded OMC list.

Programmes/formats form a separate many-to-many layer. Examples include BPCL **Ghar** highway/truck-stop outlets and **Pure for Sure**, IndianOil **XTRACARE**, HPCL **Club HP**, Jio-bp **Mobility Station/Refresh**, and Shell **Select**. An outlet can have more than one programme, and none of these labels imply COCO/CODO/DODO status.
